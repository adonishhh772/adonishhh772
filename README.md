## What I do

I turn a vague “we should do something with AI” into a defined problem, a data-readiness check, an honest prototype, an evaluation harness, and a system that knows its own limits.

- **Enterprise knowledge assistants.** Production LLM workflows, retrieval and agent orchestration on <a href="https://www.awtg.co.uk/kai" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">KAI</a> — used by 65,000+ people across major organisations, including the British Council.
- **GraphRAG for complex content.** Document intelligence that recovers structure, extracts entities and relationships, and answers with citations people can verify.
- **Decision support where mistakes are expensive.** Reinforcement learning and forecasting for telecom networks, with hard quality-of-service constraints and expert review before anything goes live.
- **The unglamorous parts.** Document quality, retrieval evaluation, prompt injection and leakage controls, cost, observability, and the governance conversations that make a system defensible.

I write about this in **<a href="https://adonishhh772.github.io/writing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Reliable AI</a>** — practical notes on agents, RAG, evaluation and enterprise delivery, without vendor noise.
